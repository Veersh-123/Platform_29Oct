User Story As an account holder of the bank, I want to log in to https://vb-bank-demo.vercel.app/ with username "john.doe" and password "user123" and, transfer USD 250 to recipient account 3456789012 with description "Towards trip to Orlando", so that my account balance is updated and the transfer appears in my transaction history when filtered for expenses and searched by "Orlando". Acceptance Criteria Successful login with username "john.doe" and password "user123" and lands the user on the account dashboard. The dashboard displays the current account balance before the transfer. Clicking the left-menu "Transfer" navigates to the "Transfer Money" screen. Entering: Recipient Account Number = 3456789012 Amount (USD) = 250 Description = "Towards trip to Orlando" and clicking the transfer button completes the transfer. After the transfer completes, the dashboard balance reflects the updated amount (original balance minus USD 250). From the left menu, clicking "History", selecting the "Expense" filter, and searching for "Orlando" returns a table row showing the recent money transferred for $250 and status as "completed"
